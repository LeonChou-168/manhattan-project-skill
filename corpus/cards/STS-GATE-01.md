## 主张

试验门的慢性死亡路径：偏差反复出现而未致灾 → 被重新归类为"非问题"（normalization of deviance）→ "reliance on past success as a substitute for sound engineering practices (such as testing to understand why systems were not performing in accordance with requirements/specifications)"。同时"organizational barriers… prevented effective communication of critical safety information and stifled professional differences of opinion"，安全组织在场但沉默："The silence of Program-level safety processes undermined oversight."信息逐级上传时被"rolled-up, abbreviated, and simplified"，弱信号在层级中消失。

## 转译

- 任何"规格外但没出事"的偏差，处置只有两种：改规格（附论证）或消除偏差。禁止第三种状态"接受着先飞"。
- 独立评审角色必须有强制发声义务：每次门评审留书面"异议/无异议"记录，沉默即失职。
- 上报摘要必须保留原始信号的量纲与不确定度，禁止只传结论。
- agent 场景：测试持续黄灯但被反复标记"已知问题"超过 N 次即自动升级为阻塞项。

## 对照

CAIB 同章以海军核潜艇 SUBSAFE 为正面对照："PowerPoint briefings and papers for technical seminars are not substitutes for completed staff work."
